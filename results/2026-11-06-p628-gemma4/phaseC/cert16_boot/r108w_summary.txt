WHY NO DETECTOR FIX CAN SAVE THE STORE ON GEMMA-4 (structural)
--------------------------------------------------------------
gemma-4-31b is a TWO-HEAD-SIZE attention hybrid (config text_config):
  50 sliding_attention layers: head_dim 256, 16 kv heads
       -> TP2 per-rank kvdim 8*256 = 2048
  10 full_attention layers: global_head_dim 512, 4 global kv heads
       -> TP2 per-rank kvdim 2*512 = 1024
The LMCache KV_2LTD memory-object is single-hidden-size by design. The
adapter builds it from model_config.get_num_kv_heads() (8 — the sliding
group) x get_head_size() (512 — the GLOBAL head dim): logged
"kv shape: (60, 2, 1024, 8, 512)" — a frankenstein 4096-wide row that
matches NEITHER pool group (2048 / 1024). With the detector guard fixed
our fast path would reshape per-layer pools to the memobj hidden and
misaddress (list branch) or wrap 2x-too-large pointer views (ptr
branch) — corruption, not correctness. Stock lmcache cannot express
this model's store at all. Conclusion: LMCache store/load on a
two-head-size attention hybrid is structurally unsupported by the
KV_2LTD format; on gemma-4 the certified posture must run ARC_LMC=0,
and the kit must FAIL LOUDLY at register time instead of wedging at
first request.
