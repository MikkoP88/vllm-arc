Mechanism amendment (this campaign)
-----------------------------------
The r108ab close-out story ("L2-bandwidth wall") is REFUTED by its own
fp16-KV control: 10.28 s (fp16) vs 10.81 s (fp8) — 2x KV bytes, same
time. A byte-bound kernel cannot be indifferent to 2x bytes. The triton
kernel is slow PER K-TILE ITERATION (~10.3 us vs 2.4 us windowed at the
same tile shape): the kernel, not the memory system, is the defect. The
engine ships a second, far faster attention kernel — vllm_xpu_kernels
ESVD FA2 (_vllm_fa2_C.abi3.so; P6.21b measured ~37 TF/s for it in qwen
production prefill) — but the triton backend has NO fa2 dispatch, so
gemma-4's hd512 full-attention layers never reach it.
