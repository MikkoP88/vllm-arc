## Source verification (image vllm-arc:v0.30.0-b70-cert4)

- `vllm/v1/attention/backends/gdn_attn.py:537` — the assert text
  (`build_for_cudagraph_capture`): requires
  `num_reqs <= decode_cudagraph_max_bs AND num_actual_tokens <= decode_cudagraph_max_bs`.
- `vllm/v1/attention/backends/gdn_attn.py:120-128` —
  `decode_cudagraph_max_bs = max_num_seqs * (num_spec + 1)` = 64 × 4 = **256**
  for our k3 posture, then `min(..., max_cudagraph_capture_size)` if set —
  i.e. 256 is a hard ceiling; declaring a LARGER max_cudagraph_capture_size
  cannot raise it.
- `vllm/config/compilation.py:1145` — `assert cudagraph_capture_sizes[-1] ==
  max_cudagraph_capture_size` (last size must equal the declared max).

## Root cause

Stock serve ladder `cudagraph_capture_sizes` topped at **320** (k4 headroom).
With `cudagraph_mode=FULL_AND_PIECEWISE`, the PIECEWISE capture list includes
the ladder top → a synthetic (num_reqs=64, num_actual_tokens=320) capture →
GDN decode-only guard rejects (320 > 256). Fix (gate1d): trim ladder to 256
(the real k3 verify shape = 64 × 4) + declare
`max_cudagraph_capture_size: 256`. Verified working: PIECEWISE 72/72 captured,
boot to health ~460 s.

\"cudagraph_capture_sizes\": [4,8,16,32,64], \"max_cudagraph_capture_size\": 64}"`
