## Link attributes — MISREPORT class (xe driver leaves defaults)
sysfs, all four devices (both GPUs AND both parent bridges):
  max=2.5 GT/s PCIe cur=2.5 GT/s PCIe width=1/1
lspci -vv LnkCap/LnkSta on the same devices: "Port #0/#8, Speed
2.5GT/s, Width x1" — same values, so it is not a sysfs-only artifact;
the kernel's PCIe link attributes for these devices simply do not
reflect the operational link.
WHY THIS CANNOT BE THE REAL LINK: U8k prefill (8,158 tokens) completes
in 3.86 s with TP2 allreduce every layer — at 250 MB/s (Gen1 x1) the
per-layer activation exchange alone would exceed the whole measured
TTFT by an order of magnitude. The r66/r67 device traces show ~GB/s
class cross-device traffic. Conclusion: misreport; the D2D probe
(b70_probe21_d2d.sh) is the decisive instrument.
